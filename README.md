# Living Spaces Elevated

LIVINT – Luxury Website Creative Brief

ROLE

You are not building a website.

You are designing the complete digital experience of a premium brand.

Think like Pentagram, Aesop, Apple, Norm Architects or Studio MK27.

The final result must feel timeless, elegant, minimal and unforgettable.

ABOUT LIVINT

LIVINT (Living Interiors) is a premium consultancy that combines Interior Design, Functional Space Planning and Classical Feng Shui to create spaces that genuinely improve the lives of the people who inhabit them.

This is NOT a Feng Shui business.

This is NOT an Interior Design studio.

This is NOT a decoration company.

LIVINT is a new category:

Living Interiors.

The philosophy is simple:

A space should not only look beautiful.

It should support the life you want to live.

The methodology combines design thinking, architecture, human behaviour, functionality and Classical Feng Shui as a technical analytical system—not as a spiritual practice.

Avoid every cliché associated with Feng Shui.

No bamboo.

No Buddhas.

No yin-yang symbols.

No dragons.

No red-and-gold palettes.

No mystical language.

No spirituality.

No lucky objects.

The brand must feel architectural, intelligent, calm and luxurious.
